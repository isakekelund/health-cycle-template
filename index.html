We live such unnatural lifestyles today that staying healthy now takes an unnatural number of deliberate actions — things a body a few generations ago never had to think about. Sleep, movement, food, light, screens, recovery — all of it now needs conscious effort just to counteract the environment we've built.

Nobody can hold all of that in their head, every day, forever. That's the whole point of this app: not to gamify your life, but to offload the remembering, so you can just do the thing.

Welcome to Health Cycle
A quick tour before you start:
  • Groups — habits are organized into sections like Morning, Evening, or your own custom ones. Edit these anytime in ⚙ Settings.
  • Functional workout — tap it to open a built-in workout tracker with Winter/Summer cycles.
  • Rest days — pick which days of the week get tracked in ⚙ Settings → Days. Everything else stays untouched, except workouts.
  • Missed habits — skipping a habit repeatedly makes it "cost" more the longer it's ignored. You choose the exact date this starts counting from in ⚙ Settings → Days.
  • Everything is editable — habits, groups, tracked days, and the workout content are all yours to change.
